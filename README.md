# 🚀 VTOP Modern — Elevate Your Student Portal

**VTOP Modern** is a browser extension (Manifest V3) that transforms the Vellore Institute of Technology student portal (**VTOP**) from an outdated legacy interface into a sleek, fast, and feature-packed modern web app.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌙 **Modern Midnight Dark Mode & Clean Light Mode**: Replaces outdated Bootstrap 3 styling with glassmorphic cards, smooth shadows, and modern typography (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- 📊 **Smart Attendance Calculator**:
  - Automatically calculates your **75% Bunk Quota** (e.g. *"Can bunk 3 classes"*).
  - Automatically calculates your **Recovery Quota** if below 75% (e.g. *"Attend next 4 classes"*).
  - Visual color-coded attendance progress bars.
  - Overall aggregate attendance summary card.
- 🕶️ **Grade & CGPA Privacy Shield**: Blurs your CGPA and marks from nosy classmates in public or computer labs. Click anytime to reveal or hide.
- ⚡ **Streamlined Login**:
  - Auto-capitalizes your Registration Number as you type.
  - Automatically focuses on the Registration Number, Password, and Captcha input fields in sequence.
  - Press `[S]` on the pre-login page to instantly jump to Student Login.
- 📅 **Timetable Enhancement**: Highlights today's schedule row automatically.
- 🎛️ **Extension Popup**: Toggle dark mode, attendance calculations, privacy blur, and access quick shortcuts (VIT Mail, Moodle LMS).

## 🛠️ Quick Installation (Takes 30 Seconds)

Works on **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, **Microsoft Edge**, **Opera**, or any Chromium browser:

1. Open your browser and go to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions/
   ```
   *(For Brave: `brave://extensions/`, For Edge: `edge://extensions/`)*

2. Toggle **Developer mode** on (top-right corner).

3. Click **"Load unpacked"** (top-left corner).

4. Select the extension directory:
   ```text
   /home/rakshit-737/vtop-modern-extension
   ```

5. Go to [https://vtopcc.vit.ac.in/vtop/open/page](https://vtopcc.vit.ac.in/vtop/open/page) and refresh the page! 🎉

## 🔒 Security & Privacy Guarantee
- **100% Client-Side**: Runs exclusively inside your browser via content scripts.
- **Zero Tracking**: Never stores or transmits your registration number, password, or academic records anywhere.
